<div class="even">
<p>Xingjiao Wu is an <b>Associate Professor</b> at the School of Pharmacy, East China Normal University (ECNU), where he leads research on <b>AI4Science, digital therapeutics, and human-in-the-loop computing</b>. He received his Ph.D. in Computer Application Technology from ECNU in 2022 (advisor: Prof. Liang He), and was a Shanghai Super Postdoctoral Fellow at Fudan University (2022–2024, advisor: Prof. Cheng Jin). He has published 60+ papers in international journals and conferences, including 1 ESI Hot Paper and 3 ESI Highly Cited Papers, with works at ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, ACM MM, AAAI, IJCAI, ACL, and top journals such as Information Fusion and IP&amp;M. His work received the Best Paper Award at McGE'23 and the Editor's Choice recognition at FGCS, and contributed to the <b>First Prize of the 2022 Shanghai Technological Invention Award</b> and the <b>Second Prize of the Shanghai Sci-Tech Progress Award</b>. He currently PI's the NSFC Young Scientists Fund project on digital therapeutics for depression (2025–2028).</p>
</div>

<h2 class="hh">Research Interests</h2>
<div class="even">
<p><b>1) Emotion-controllable personalized multimodal digital therapeutics generation</b> — AIGC of music, video, image, and EEG content that aligns with an individual's real-time affective state, toward alleviating anxiety and depression.<br><br>
<b>2) Inverse-RL-based human-in-the-loop computing</b> — small-sample key-representation mining and human-machine knowledge fusion for self-evolving domain agents, with applications to embodied intelligence and chronic-disease prediction.<br><br>
<b>3) Deep learning for complex document layout analysis</b> — generation, mining, and evaluation of complex layouts (magazines, ancient books, and medical texts); former collaboration with Huawei Noah's Ark Lab, topping the InfographicVQA benchmark (Nov 2023).</p>
</div>
